About RIBPI

Founded in 2025 by Dan Hersey. Nonpartisan. Rhode Island-first. Research and education on Bitcoin and digital asset policy.

Our Mission

RIBPI works with Rhode Island lawmakers on Bitcoin and digital asset policy. We draft bills, write fiscal analyses, and deliver committee testimony. The work is nonpartisan and Rhode Island-first. We currently operate as an unincorporated grassroots research initiative.

Leadership

Dan Hersey

dan@ribpi.com · @RIBTCPolicy

Funding & status

RIBPI is currently unincorporated and operates as a grassroots research and education initiative. No outside funding, no paid lobbyists, no political contributions. If we incorporate or accept funding in the future, we will publish that here within seven days.

What We Do

Three pillars of the Rhode Island Bitcoin Policy Initiative

Education & outreach

Briefings with Rhode Island lawmakers and their staff. We sit with committee members one-on-one and answer the questions that come up between hearings. Six bills in the current session can be traced to conversations that started over coffee.

Drafting & testimony

S.2021, H.7956, H.7957, H.7413, and H.7843 each carry RIBPI material in some form. Dan delivered live testimony on every one this session. We also wrote the redline opposing H.7955 and S.2648 from the same policy seat.

Policy research

White papers and one-pagers, every claim sourced. Material lives on the Research page and is used by bill sponsors, opposition counsel, and statehouse reporters.

Our Timeline

Milestones in the Rhode Island Bitcoin Policy Initiative

2025

RIBPI founded by Dan Hersey. First conversations with RI legislators on Bitcoin policy.

S.0451 (de minimis tax exemption) drafted with sponsor; S.0373 (blockchain study commission) passed the Senate 38–0 but never received a House vote.

January – April 2026

Six 2026 bills introduced with RIBPI involvement: S.2021, H.7956 + S.2198, H.7957 + S.2196, H.7413, H.7843, plus a policy brief opposing H.7955 + S.2648 kiosk expansion.

Dan delivered live testimony on each of the six. Coverage in RI Current, Bitcoin Magazine, Route Fifty, CoinCentral, and others.

June 2026

S.2198 Sub A reported out of Senate AI & Emerging Technology with a recommendation to pass. Senate floor vote scheduled for June 10.

S.2021 hearing scheduled for June 8 in Senate Finance.
