May 7, 2026 Bitcoin Magazine

Making Impacts in Bitcoin Policy at the State Level | Bitcoin 2026

Dan Hersey joined Colin Crossman (State of Wyoming) and Dana Criswell (Young Americans for Liberty) for a 26-minute panel at the Bitcoin 2026 conference, moderated by Dom Bei (Proof of Workforce). The panel walks through state-level Bitcoin legislation across 40+ states, the strongest privacy and self-custody bills, and the case for and against state Bitcoin reserves — with RIBPI’s Rhode Island work as a recurring reference point.
