Enacted law

SECTION 2. Virtual currency kiosk regulation. Signed June 23, 2025, the law licenses kiosk operators under the Department of Business Regulation. It sets daily limits of $2,000 for new customers and $5,000 for existing customers, and it requires fee disclosure, on-screen fraud warnings, blockchain analytics, a compliance officer and refunds for scam victims. It does not cap fees. No kiosk bill passed in 2026.

RIBPI’s position: concerns. Kiosk scams are a real problem and they fall hardest on older Rhode Islanders. Our concern is with how the law goes about it. On-screen warnings resemble the gift-card warnings that did not stop gift-card fraud. The cost of other people’s crimes falls on operators, and the law has no provision aimed at the person running the scam. How it should change is one of the questions we will bring to the study commission.
