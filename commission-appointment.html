Press ReleaseSenate President Lawson appoints RIBPI founder Dan Hersey to the study commission on blockchain and cryptocurrency

For immediate release November 21, 2026

PROVIDENCE, R.I., November 21, 2026. Senate President Valarie J. Lawson has appointed Dan Hersey, founder of the Rhode Island Bitcoin Policy Initiative (RIBPI), to the Special Legislative Commission to Study Blockchain and Cryptocurrency. The appointment, dated November 9, 2026, names Hersey to the seat reserved for a representative with experience and expertise in cryptocurrency, one of five seats on the commission held by members of the public.

The Senate created the commission on June 10, 2026, when it adopted S.2198 Sub A by a vote of 38–0. Sen. Louis P. DiPalma was the lead sponsor. The commission has ten seats. Three go to senators, one to the Secretary of Commerce and one to the Director of the Department of Business Regulation. The other five go to members of the public with backgrounds in academia, banking, federal securities law, blockchain technology and cryptocurrency.

The resolution directs the commission to review how blockchain and cryptocurrency are used and regulated around the country, determine where Rhode Island law stands today, study non-fungible tokens, hear from industry experts, and recommend legislation that supports economic growth along with appropriate consumer protections. Its final report goes to the General Assembly, the Secretary of Commerce and the General Treasurer by January 5, 2028. The commission is expected to begin meeting soon.

“I have spent a lot of time at the State House answering legislators’ questions about Bitcoin, and most of them were fair questions that nobody had sat down and answered before,” Hersey said. “The commission gives Rhode Island a place to work through those questions with the record from other states in front of it. I am grateful to President Lawson for the appointment and to Senator DiPalma for carrying the resolution through the Senate, and I am looking forward to getting to work with the other members.”

Hersey founded RIBPI in 2025. He drafts its bills, writes its fiscal analyses and delivers its testimony, and in the 2026 session he testified before four House and Senate committees on every bill RIBPI tracked, including the resolution that created the commission.

RIBPI will keep publishing its research, state comparisons and briefing materials on this site, so that commissioners, legislators and the public are all reading the same documents. Its agenda for the 2027 session puts education for the commission and for lawmakers ahead of the three bills it will ask legislators to introduce.

About RIBPI. The Rhode Island Bitcoin Policy Initiative is a nonpartisan research and education organization that works with Rhode Island lawmakers on Bitcoin and digital asset policy. It briefs legislators and their staff, drafts bills, writes fiscal analyses and testifies in committee. RIBPI has no outside funding and no paid lobbyists, and it makes no political contributions.
