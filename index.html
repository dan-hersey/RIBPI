Bitcoin Policy for the Ocean State

The Rhode Island Bitcoin Policy Initiative drafts bills, writes fiscal analyses, and delivers testimony on digital asset policy at the Rhode Island General Assembly. Nonpartisan. Founded in 2025 by Dan Hersey.

2026 Active Legislation

Four bills we've drafted or helped introduce in the Rhode Island General Assembly this session · status as of June 6, 2026

S2198 Sub A
Senate Floor · Jun 10

Blockchain Study Commission

Reported out of committee June 2; on Senate Calendar for floor vote June 10. Sub A creates a 10-member commission to study blockchain applications for voting, land registries, public records, and economic development.
S2021
Senate Finance · Hearing Jun 8

De Minimis Tax Exemption

Tax-free Bitcoin payments up to $5,000/month ($20,000/year) to reduce friction for everyday use. Hearing scheduled for June 8 in Senate Finance.
H7957 / S2196
Held for Further Study

Private Key Protection

Protects citizens from being compelled to disclose private cryptographic keys in legal proceedings. H.7957 held Mar 5; S.2196 held Apr 7.
H7413
Held for Further Study

Economic Growth Blockchain Act

Regulatory sandbox and Special Purpose Depository Institutions to make RI a blockchain innovation hub. Held for further study Feb 3.
