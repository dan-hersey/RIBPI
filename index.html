The 2027 agenda

Education for the Senate’s study commission comes first, alongside three bills RIBPI will ask legislators to introduce · status as of November 17, 2026

S2198 Sub A
Created June 10, 2026

Study Commission Education

The Senate created a 10-member commission to study blockchain and cryptocurrency. Briefing its members and other lawmakers is RIBPI’s first job in 2027. Interim report due January 5, 2027; final report January 5, 2028.
2026: H7957 / S2196
Returning in 2027

Private Key Protection

A court or agency could not force someone to hand over a private key when a public key or the asset itself would do. Wyoming (2023) and Utah (2024) have this law.
2026: S2021
Revised for 2027

Bitcoin Payments De Minimis

Small everyday purchases made with bitcoin would no longer trigger a state income tax calculation. Redrafted to answer the Division of Taxation’s June 2026 letter on S.2021.
Ch. 33-21.1
New for 2027

Unclaimed Digital Assets

Abandoned bitcoin would reach the General Treasurer as bitcoin, the way abandoned stock arrives as stock, so the owner can get the asset back. Virginia, Maine, Utah and Alabama enacted this in 2026.
